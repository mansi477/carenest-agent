# Appointments and Consultations

## How to book a consultation
Open the CareNest app, tap Consult, choose a specialty and pick an available slot. You can also ask the support assistant to book for you. Video and in-clinic consultations are available for most specialties.

## Rescheduling an appointment
You can reschedule up to 2 hours before the start time at no charge from Appointments, then Manage. Each appointment can be rescheduled twice. Within 2 hours of the start time, rescheduling is not available.

## Cancelling a consultation and consultation refunds
If you cancel more than 4 hours before the start time, you get a full refund. If you cancel within 4 hours of the start time, you get a 50% refund. No-shows are not refunded. Refund timelines are covered under billing and refunds.

## What to keep ready for a video consultation
Use a stable internet connection and a quiet room. Keep your previous prescriptions and reports handy. The join link appears in the app 10 minutes before the start time.

## If the doctor does not join
If the doctor has not joined within 10 minutes of the start time, you can either reschedule for free or request a full refund from the appointment page.

## Follow-up consultations
You get one free follow-up with the same doctor within 7 days of your consultation, for the same concern.
