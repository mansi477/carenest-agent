# Billing, Refunds, Privacy and Support

## Payment methods
CareNest accepts UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking and CareNest Wallet.

## Refund timelines
Refunds to the original payment method take 5 to 7 business days. Refunds to CareNest Wallet are instant.

## Failed payments
If money was debited but your booking was not confirmed, the amount is reversed automatically within 3 business days. If it has not reversed after that, contact support with the transaction ID.

## Invoices and GST
Download invoices from Orders or Appointments, then Invoice. GST invoices can be generated by adding your GSTIN in Settings before placing the order.

## How we handle your health data
Your health records are encrypted and shared only with the doctor you consult. You can request deletion of your data from Settings, then Privacy. Deletion requests are completed within 30 days.

## Contacting support
Support is available from 8 AM to 10 PM IST, every day. Chat messages get a first response within 15 minutes. You can ask for a human agent at any time.
