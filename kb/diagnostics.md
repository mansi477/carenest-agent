# Diagnostics and Lab Tests

## Booking home sample collection
Choose your tests, pick a time slot and confirm your address. A phlebotomist arrives during your 2-hour slot window and calls you before arriving.

## Fasting instructions
Some tests, such as fasting blood sugar and lipid profile, need 8 to 12 hours of fasting. Plain water is allowed. The exact instructions for your tests are in your booking confirmation.

## Report turnaround time
Most blood test reports are ready within 24 hours of sample collection. Specialised tests can take up to 48 hours. Reports appear under Reports in the app and are also emailed to you.

## Rescheduling sample collection
You can reschedule a sample collection for free up to 1 hour before the start of your slot.

## Health checkup packages
Every health checkup package includes a doctor consultation to discuss your reports. Book that consultation within 7 days of receiving your reports.
