# Medicine Delivery

## Delivery timelines
In metro cities, medicine orders placed before 4 PM are delivered the same day. In other cities, delivery takes 2 to 3 days. You can track your order from Orders, then Track.

## Prescriptions for medicines
Prescription-only medicines need a valid prescription. Upload one, or use the prescription from a CareNest consultation. A pharmacist verifies it, usually within 30 minutes. Orders cannot ship until the prescription is verified.

## Delivery charges
Delivery is free on orders above Rs 499. Orders below that amount have a delivery charge of Rs 40.

## Wrong, damaged or missing items
Report the problem within 24 hours of delivery from Orders, then Report an issue, and attach a photo of the item and the packaging. We will arrange a replacement or a refund after review.

## Returning medicines
Medicines cannot be returned once delivered, unless the item is damaged, wrong, or expired. Opened strips and bottles are not accepted for return.
