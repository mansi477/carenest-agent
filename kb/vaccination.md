# Vaccinations

## Booking a vaccination appointment
Tap Vaccination, choose the vaccine, pick a vaccination centre and select a slot. CareNest does not decide which vaccines you or your child need. Please follow your doctor's advice or the national immunisation schedule.

## What to carry to the centre
Carry a government photo ID and your previous vaccination records or vaccination card. For children, carry the child's vaccination card and the parent's photo ID.

## After your vaccination
Stay at the centre for 30 minutes after the vaccination so staff can observe you. Tell the centre staff right away if you feel unwell.

## Vaccination certificates
Your vaccination certificate appears under Reports within 24 hours of the vaccination.

## Missed vaccination appointment
If you miss your vaccination appointment, you can reschedule it once for free from the appointment page.
